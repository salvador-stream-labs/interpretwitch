Interpretwitch is installed.

Open OBS and connect Twitch in Settings → Stream.
Open Docks → Chat, then Tools → Interpretwitch to choose a language and download a model.

Translations appear privately in your Twitch Chat dock.

To remove the plugin, quit OBS and remove awl-chat.plugin from:
~/Library/Application Support/obs-studio/plugins

Previous installations are kept in:
~/Library/Application Support/obs-studio/awl-plugin-backups
