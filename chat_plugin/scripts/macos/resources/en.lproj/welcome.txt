Interpretwitch by Salvador Stream Labs

Private, local translation in OBS's Twitch Chat dock.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later, and OBS 32.2.2 in Applications.
Quit OBS completely with Command-Q before continuing.

Installs for your Mac account only. No administrator password is needed.
An existing plugin is backed up. Your models and settings are kept.
Choose and download a translation model from Interpretwitch's settings after installation.
